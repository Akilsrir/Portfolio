A chessboard that moves its own pieces Fits this role

Chess eXecution Robotic Assembly (CXRA)

You play against it like a normal board. A camera reads your move, a chess engine picks a reply, and a motorized gantry carries an electromagnet to the piece and moves it to its new square.

I modeled the actuator in SolidWorks and wrote the control logic as six steps that run in order. Splitting it up that way meant I could test and fix one step at a time. Press the button to watch one move.

  • Idle
  • Scan
  • Validate
  • Compute
  • Execute
  • Reset

Built with stepper motors, belt drives, an electromagnet and an ESP32. I'm currently testing the magnet's switching circuit on a separate rig.

The arm is parked in the corner.
